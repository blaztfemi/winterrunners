| Official explanation first given as **Venus**, later revised to a **Skyhook** balloon from a then-classified program | VERIFIED | U.S. Army; [Military.com](https://www.military.com/history/first-air-force-pilot-die-chasing-ufo-was-actually-chasing-secret-balloon.html) |

> **Why this lands:** it requires no invention. A U.S. Army colonel in
> Eric's home county reported a light that *held still*, a man died, and
> the official explanation provably changed because the real one was
> classified. The site never claims the Army knows what Eric saw — it
> shows that the one time a light over Hardin County got a top-level
> investigation, the public answer had to be corrected.
